Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect the information you provide, such as your name and email address. As you use the Service, we also collect the expense records you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to provide and improve the Service — for example, to display your expense history, generate category breakdowns and monthly summaries, and keep your account secure. We do not use your personal financial data for advertising, and we do not sell your data to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely and retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time, after which your data will be permanently removed from our systems within a reasonable period.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on third-party infrastructure providers, such as hosting and database services, to operate the Service. These providers process data solely on our behalf and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes. We do not share your expense data with advertisers or data brokers.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on your account page.
